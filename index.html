About RepairAI

RepairAI is an AI-powered advisor that helps you decide whether a damaged product is worth repairing. Describe your product and the problem, and RepairAI gives you a repairability score, the likely cause, the parts that may need replacing, a rough cost in rupees, step-by-step repair guidance, and a clear repair-or-replace recommendation.

Every product that is repaired instead of thrown away keeps electronic waste out of landfill and money in your pocket.

What you get

Everything you need to make a confident decision about a damaged product, in one place.

📊 AI AnalysisA repairability score out of 100 with the reasons behind it.
🧩 Cost & PartsThe likely issue, the parts to replace, and a rough cost range.
🛠️ Repair GuideSimple step-by-step instructions, with safety tips.
⚖️ Repair vs ReplaceA side-by-side comparison so you can decide with confidence.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your product: what it is, how old it is, and what is wrong.
  2. Get your AI repair assessment: score, likely cause, parts and cost.
  3. Decide and save: compare repair with replace, then keep the result in your history.

The project

RepairAI follows a simple input, process, output pattern. The score comes from seven repairability checks, each marked 1 to 5 and weighted: opening the product (25%), replacing the part that fails most (20%), reaching common parts (15%), spare parts availability (15%), repair guides (10%), software or lock-in blocks (10%) and special tools (5%).
